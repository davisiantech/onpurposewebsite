Privacy Policy

Last updated May 2026

Introduction

Welcome to the website of On Purpose Young Adults, a young adult ministry of the Calhoun Seventh-day Adventist Church. We value your privacy and are committed to keeping this online space safe, transparent, and free of invasive data collection.

Simply put, we do not collect, track, or sell your personal data.

Information we do not collect

You do not need to sign up, log in, or provide personal details to view this site.

  • No contact forms. We do not host contact forms or newsletter signup boxes on our domain.
  • No cookies. We do not write or use cookies to track your browsing behavior.
  • No first-party tracking. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tracking tags.

Third-party links and services

Our website connects you with events and community resources hosted by other services.

  • Google Forms. Some event registration links open an external Google Form. Information you choose to submit is collected directly by Google and managed through the relevant church or event organizer account.
  • Google Calendar. Event-detail links may open Google Calendar, which operates under Google's own privacy practices.
  • Google Maps. Location links open Google Maps to help you find meeting places. Google may use location information or cookies when you use its service.
  • Instagram. Gallery links open our Instagram profile or individual posts. Instagram may collect information according to its own privacy policy.
  • AdventistGiving. Donation links open the official church donation platform. We do not process, receive, or store financial or payment information.

Email communication

If you contact us at info@onpurposeya.com, we use your email address and message only to respond or assist you. We do not sell your email address.
