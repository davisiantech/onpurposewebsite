About On Purpose

“In all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose.”
Romans 8:28

We’re young adults making room for real friendship, honest conversation, and a faith that shows up in everyday life.

Connect. Grow. Serve.

What we believe

Faith is a journey.

Jesus at the Center

He is the lens through which we see God, the world, and ourselves. In His life, death, and resurrection, we find our ultimate purpose.

The Word as Our Map

The Bible is more than an old book. It’s a living invitation to know God’s heart and find a better way to live.

Rest & Restoration

We cherish the Sabbath as a gift of rhythm in a restless world. It reminds us that God's love, not productivity, defines us.

Hope for Tomorrow

We believe Jesus is coming back to make all things new. That hope shapes how we live on purpose today.
